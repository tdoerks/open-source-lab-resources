# Phage-Rich Organism Study Series

## Overview

A comprehensive analysis series focusing on organisms with **high prophage burdens** to study prophage-plasmid-AMR interactions across different bacterial pathogens, including both aerobic and anaerobic species.

## Completed Studies

### 1. Pseudomonas aeruginosa Phage Hunter ✅
- **Status**: COMPLETED
- **Samples**: ~3,750 genomes (50/month, Jan 2020 - Mar 2026)
- **Prophage burden**: **5-10 prophages/genome** (HIGHEST)
- **Focus**: Temporal phage-plasmid-AMR dynamics
- **Metabolism**: Aerobic
- **Location**: `pseudomonas_phage_hunter_monthly/`

### 2. Vibrio cholerae Geographic + Temporal ✅

## Research Questions Across Studies

### Shared Questions (All 5 Studies)
1. How does prophage prevalence vary across bacterial species?
2. What are plasmid-prophage co-occurrence patterns?
3. How do AMR genes move (chromosome vs plasmid vs prophage)?
4. Can we detect phage-mediated HGT events?
5. How does XDR/MDR emergence relate to mobile elements?

### Organism-Specific Questions

#### Pseudomonas
- Why does Pseudomonas have the highest prophage burden?
- Do prophages contribute to biofilm formation?
- Phage-plasmid interactions in CF isolates?

#### Vibrio
- CTXφ prophage prevalence by geographic region?
- Temporal epidemic waves per region?
- SXT/R391 ICE distribution across endemic zones?

#### Salmonella
- Do Typhimurium strains carry more prophages than Enteritidis?
- Are Gifsy-1/2 prophages still prevalent in modern Typhimurium?
- Does prophage content correlate with MDR by serotype?

#### Fusobacterium (NEW)
- **How many prophages does F. necrophorum carry (compared to aerobes)?**
- **Do subspecies differ in prophage burden (*necrophorum* vs *funduliforme*)?**
- **Are there prophage-encoded virulence factors (leukotoxin on mobile elements)?**
- **Do bovine and human isolates differ in prophage profiles?**
- **Is prophage biology different in obligate anaerobes vs aerobes?**
- **Does F. necrophorum show evidence of phage-mediated AMR transfer?**

## Metabolism Comparison: Aerobic vs Anaerobic Prophage Biology

### Hypothesis
**Anaerobic bacteria may have different prophage dynamics** due to:
- Different replication strategies
- Oxygen-independent phage biology
- Lower environmental phage pressure (no aerobic environments)
- Different stress responses affecting lysogeny

### Test with F. necrophorum
- **First obligate anaerobe** in series
- Compare prophage burden to aerobic/facultative species
- Assess prophage diversity in anaerobic environment
- **Publication angle**: "Prophage dynamics differ between aerobic and anaerobic bacterial pathogens"
